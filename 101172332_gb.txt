Institution Overview:
George Brown Polytechnic is a fully accredited public college of applied arts and technology. 
It is located in Toronto, Ontario, Canada.

Key Campuses:
1. St. James Campus: Located in downtown Toronto, primarily hosting business, community services, and creative arts programs.
2. Casa Loma Campus: Situated at the edge of the Annex, known for technology, construction, and engineering programs.
3. Waterfront Campus: Located at Cherry Street, specializing in health sciences.

Academic Focus:
George Brown College is recognized for its career-focused education, offering a wide array of diplomas, 
post-graduate certificates, and degree programs in many different and highly relevent fields.
