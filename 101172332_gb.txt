Institution Overview:
George Brown College of Applied Arts and Technology is a fully accredited public college of applied arts and technology 
located in Toronto, Ontario, Canada.

Key Campuses:
- St. James Campus: Located in downtown Toronto, primarily hosting business, community services, and creative arts programs.
- Casa Loma Campus: Situated at the edge of the Annex, known for technology, construction, and engineering programs.
- Waterfront Campus: Located at Cherry Street, specializing in health sciences.

Academic Focus:
George Brown College is recognized for its career-focused education, offering a wide array of diplomas, 
post-graduate certificates, and degree programs in fields such as Computer Programming and Analysis, 
system design, and software development, closely connected with Toronto's tech industry.
