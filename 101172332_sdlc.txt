The Importance of Learning DevOps in the SDLC:

Bridging the Silos:
DevOps integrates development and operations, fostering shared responsibility for product delivery and system reliability.

Accelerating Time-to-Market:
Automated CI/CD pipelines reduce manual bottlenecks, enabling faster feature and bug fix releases to production.

Improving Software Quality and Stability:
Automated testing and continuous monitoring catch defects early in the SDLC before they reach production.

Scalability and Repeatability:
Standardized configurations and build processes ensure consistent application behavior across all environments.
