The Importance of Learning DevOps in the SDLC:

1. Bridging the Silos:
DevOps integrates development and operations. It fosters shared responsibility for product delivery and system reliability.

2. Accelerating Time-to-Market:
Automated CI/CD pipelines reduce manual bottlenecks. It enables faster feature and bug fix releases to production.

3. Improving Software Quality and Stability:
Automated testing and continuous monitoring catch defects early in the SDLC before they reach production. This can also result 
in huge cost savings. 

4. Scalability and Repeatability:
Standardized configurations and build processes ensure consistent application behavior across all environments.
