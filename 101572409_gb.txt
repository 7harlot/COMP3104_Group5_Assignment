George Brown College
====================
Written by: Ben Morrison - 101572409

Was established in 1967, and began operations in 1968.

There are three primary campuses: St. James, Casa Loma, Waterfront.

George Brown has roughly 29,000+ full-time students, 2000+ part-time students, and over 53,000 continuing education registrants.

George Brown Polytechnic core disciplines: Arts, Design & Information Technology, Community Services, Health Sciences, Hospitality & Culinary Arts, and Construction & Engineering Technologies.
