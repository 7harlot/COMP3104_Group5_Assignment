COMP3104 - DevOps
==================
Written by: Ben Morrison - 101572409

DevOps culture brings the cohesion of autonomous teams to fast paced, high-demanding environments.

It can drastically reduce IT overhead. Remove manual tasks both of a mundane or complex nature. Enforce best security practices. Expedite product delivery. Improve communication and collaboration across teams.

DevOps allows for engineers to optimize the environment that they are working in.

Woo, let's go DevOps~! \o/
